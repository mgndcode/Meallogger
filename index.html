Log a day

Type what you ate in Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner and tap Save. You can put each item on its own line. Clear removes the saved meal. Daily % (0–100) and Weight (kg) are one entry per day, each with its own Save button.

Favourites

Add to Fav saves the text in the box to your favourites, kept in A–Z order. ★ Favourites lets you tick several and add them to the box. Manage the list in the Favourite meals card.

Go to another day

Tap the green Today ▾ bar to open the calendar and pick any date, or use the ‹ › arrows. Dark green days have all 3 meals, light green days have some.

Notes, Summary and Weight trend

Open them with Show. Notes holds one note per day. Summary shows streaks and averages. Weight trend charts your weight.

Records (red box)

  • Search finds every day you ate a keyword. It also searches notes.
  • Export makes an Excel or PDF file for a week, month, year, all records or custom dates.
  • Import restores data from an Excel file exported by this app.
  • Delete removes a date, a date range or everything. Deleted days stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and you can tap Restore.

Buttons

Save, Excel and PDF are grey. A green border means it is working and a fully green button means it is done. If you edit the text again, Save turns grey.

Back up your data

Your entries are stored only on this phone. About once a week, export All records as Excel and keep the file in Files or iCloud. If your data is ever lost, use Import to restore it.

Not showing the latest version?

Close the app fully and reopen it. If it still looks old, open the web address once in Safari and refresh. Don’t clear Safari’s website data, because that deletes your entries.

MealLog v1.0

A simple personal meal log for iPhone: meals, Daily %, weight, notes, search and exports.

Your data

Everything is stored on this device, in the browser’s local storage. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere. Removing the app from the Home Screen or clearing website data erases it, so keep your backups.
